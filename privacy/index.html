One Sweep
Privacy Policy.

One Sweep is designed to work without collecting personal data from the app.

Publisher

One Sweep is published by Amaan Hussain under the independent studio brand Ketchup with Technology.

Data collection

One Sweep does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising, remote analytics, tracking, user accounts, or social features.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, scores, settings, gameplay records, and earned constellation information are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the game and is not transmitted to Ketchup with Technology.

Deleting the app may delete this locally stored information.

Children’s privacy

One Sweep does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message. We use that information only to respond to your request and maintain necessary support records. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Changes to this policy

If One Sweep’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to support@ketchupwithtechnology.com.
